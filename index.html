Angular Prep
Privacy Policy

For Angular Prep: Learn & Quiz
Android package: com.toolifyaistudio.angularprep
Effective date: 9 November 2026

Toolify Ai Studio develops Angular Prep, an educational app for learning Angular and preparing for interviews. This policy describes the data handling of the app's current release.

Information handled by the app

Angular Prep does not require an account. It saves your lesson completion, bookmarks, flashcard review schedule, quiz answers and scores, activity dates, last lesson, and theme preference on your device so you can continue learning. The app does not send this learning information to Toolify Ai Studio.

Collection, sharing, and advertising

The current release does not include advertising, third-party analytics, a developer-operated backend, or automatic crash-reporting services. We do not collect, sell, or share your learning records. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location.

External links

If you open an official documentation link or this policy, your browser connects to the external website. Those websites and their hosting providers process requests under their own privacy policies and may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use those messages to respond to your request and retain them only as needed for support or applicable obligations. Please avoid including passwords or other sensitive information. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence.

Retention and deletion

Your learning records remain on your device until you clear the app's storage through Android settings or uninstall the app. There is no server-side learning account to delete. Android backup is disabled in the app configuration; device-specific transfer behavior may depend on your device manufacturer. We cannot recover learning records after they are removed.

Security

Your learning information is kept in the app's device storage. Device access controls help protect it; no storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not include sensitive personal information in support messages unless necessary.

Children

The app is intended for people learning software development and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information through support.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and revise its effective date. Any future account, analytics, advertising, or cloud-sync features would require a corresponding update before release.
